About

I'm a PhD candidate in the Department of Economic Sciences at IIT Kanpur. I hold an MA in Economics from the Delhi School of Economics and a BA (Hons.) in Economics from S.G.T.B. Khalsa College, University of Delhi.

My work sits at the intersection of data-driven research and public policy, using econometric methods, time series analysis, and forecasting to study real-world economic questions. I've worked as a Project Associate at the Just Transition Research Centre, IIT Kanpur, on the repurposing of closed coal mines under Coal India Limited, and as an intern with the Department of Economic Affairs, Ministry of Finance, on public-private partnership infrastructure projects. I've also spent time at EY India analysing export–import trends in the automobile parts industry.

I work primarily in R, Stata, and Python, and I'm comfortable across the applied toolkit — from probit and IV models to ARIMA forecasting.
